OUR STORY

How Mayoral became one of Egbeda's most-loved places to stay, eat, and unwind.

location_on Unity Estate, Egbeda
Built on hospitality

AN INTIMATE
HOTEL.

Mayoral Hotels & Suites was built on a simple idea — that a great stay shouldn't require a great compromise. Our guests come for the quiet street, the spotless rooms, and a front-desk team that remembers your name by the second visit.

Tucked into Unity Estate, just off the main road in Egbeda, we're close enough to the action to make business easy, and far enough back that the city's noise stays outside. Whether you're here for one night or one week, we'd like you to feel less like a guest and more like a regular.

Downstairs, our kitchen and bar carry the same idea: take the classics seriously, treat every guest like a friend, and never serve anything we wouldn't eat ourselves.
